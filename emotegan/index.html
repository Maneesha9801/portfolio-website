EMOTEGAN

Synthesizing high-fidelity, emotion-aware digital identities using Generative Adversarial Networks.

The Challenge

Digital avatars often suffer from the "Uncanny Valley" effect—lacking genuine emotional depth and micro-expressions that make human interaction feel real.

The Tech

A custom GAN architecture incorporating Squeeze-and-Excitation networks and Eye-Attention loss functions to preserve gaze direction and identity.

The Impact

Achieved a 15% improvement in Mean Opinion Score (MOS) over baseline models, creating avatars suitable for VR, gaming, and telepresence.

Interface
Design

The EmoteGAN interface was designed to demystify the complex underlying AI. By adopting a "Living Identity" metaphor, we transformed abstract parameters into tangible controls.

Design
Strategy

The "Dead Eye" Problem

Early GAN models often generated faces with "dead eyes"—lacking focus or realistic reflections. This destroys user trust instantly.

Solution: Eye-Attention Loss

We implemented a specific loss function that penalizes the model heavily for artifacts in the eye region, forcing it to prioritize gaze coherence.

Trust through Transparency

Deepfakes are scary. To mitigate anxiety, the UI explicitly labels generated content and shows the "confidence" metrics of the AI.

  • Visible "Generated" watermarks
  • Real-time rendering status indicators
  • Clear separation between "Source" and "Output"

Engineering

The core of EmoteGAN is a modified StarGAN architecture. We introduced a "Squeeze-and-Excitation" (SE) block into the generator, allowing the model to adaptively recalibrate channel-wise feature responses.
